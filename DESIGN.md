# Viewport Atlas visual system
The workspace is a precise inspection bench: a dark navy-green navigation rail, cool off-white canvas, white instruments, and turquoise selected states. The supplied logo anchors the rail. Product controls use a system sans serif, compact labels, generous grouping, and legible contrast. Synthetic preview content has its own warm outdoor editorial identity so the tool and inspected page are unmistakable.

Operate mode: navigation → address and synchronization → device canvas → audit settings → evidence review. At narrow widths the rail becomes a top bar, the audit panel stacks, and device previews scroll horizontally. Use 4/8px spacing, 1px cool-gray borders, 8px control radii, and a 12px panel radius. Coral indicates attention; turquoise indicates active controls; all statuses include text. Device viewport dimensions remain independent of visual zoom. Evidence and limitations stay visible across reports, history, and integrations.

Light is the default theme. The top-bar sun/moon toggle switches the whole product interface to a dark theme and persists `atlas-theme` locally. Dark mode uses navy-green canvas and elevated surfaces, pale text, turquoise selected states, and distinct warning/error surfaces; it does not recolor captured websites or exported evidence. Semantic dark tokens and component mappings live in `src/themes.css`. An extension-compatible local head script restores the saved preference before first paint.
